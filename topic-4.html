Indie Game Incubator & Hub

Independent game studios drive original game mechanics and artistic risks across the gaming industry. Our hub connects solo developers directly with early testers, community funding, and cloud distribution pipelines.

Early Playtesting Communities

Developers upload early prototype builds directly onto cloud streaming nodes, allowing instant browser playtesting without manual installation files. Gamers provide time-stamped feedback, crash reports, and balancing suggestions straight to development teams.

Bypassing traditional publisher royalty models allows players to back promising projects directly. Supporting studios grants early alpha access keys, exclusive in-game cosmetic rewards, and direct credits recognition upon official project launch.
